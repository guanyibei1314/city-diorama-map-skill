# Negative Prompt

```text
cartoon, anime, flat map, plain infographic, low poly, fantasy city, futuristic sci-fi city, neon cyberpunk, toy plastic look, cheap miniature, duplicated landmarks, repeated buildings, distorted architecture, floating buildings, impossible bridges, bridge not connected to land, warped coastline, broken roads, random mountains, inaccurate airport layout, runway crossing buildings, oversized airplanes, random ships, excessive blur, heavy fog, oversaturated colors, harsh HDR, fisheye distortion, chaotic labels, gibberish text, malformed Chinese characters, watermark, logo, border, frame, low detail, noisy texture
```

## 中文约束

- 不要卡通、动漫、低多边形风格。
- 不要普通平面地图或信息图。
- 不要霓虹科幻城市。
- 不要塑料玩具感。
- 不要重复地标。
- 不要让桥梁悬空、机场跑道穿过建筑、港口脱离海岸。
- 不要过度虚化主体。
- 不要堆满无意义文字。
